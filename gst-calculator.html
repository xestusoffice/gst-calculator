Mathematical Mechanics of GST Calculation

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a destination-based, multi-stage indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. Calculating GST accurately requires understanding whether the quoted price is tax-exclusive (pre-tax base) or tax-inclusive (post-tax retail price).

1. GST Exclusive Formula (Adding GST)

When the stated price does not include tax, GST is calculated by applying the statutory tax rate directly to the net taxable base:

GST Amount = (Base Amount × GST Rate) / 100
Gross Price = Base Amount + GST Amount

2. GST Inclusive Formula (Extracting / Reverse GST)

When an item is sold as "inclusive of all taxes" (MRP), the tax amount embedded inside the gross price is extracted using reverse division:

GST Amount = Gross Price - [Gross Price × {100 / (100 + GST Rate)}]
Net Base Amount = Gross Price - GST Amount
