Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

At XESTUS Finance & Tax Suite ("we", "our", or "us"), protecting the financial privacy and confidentiality of our users is the core architectural principle upon which our platform is engineered.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you access and use our website and web applications hosted at gst.xestus.in and related subdomains, in full compliance with:

  • India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679)
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

1. The 100% In-Browser Execution Architecture

Unlike conventional software platforms, we do not collect, process, transmit, or store any of your financial data on our servers.

When you enter values into our GST Calculator, GST Invoice Generator, Income Tax Calculator, Loan EMI Amortizer, or SIP Wealth Planner:

  • Every mathematical operation is executed solely inside your web browser’s local sandbox memory.
  • No invoice line items, client names, GSTIN numbers, bank account details, or salary brackets are ever transmitted to or logged on any external server.
  • Drafts saved via the "Save Draft" button in the Invoice Generator are stored strictly in your browser's local HTML5 localStorage database on your physical device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your Aadhaar number, PAN card number, bank credentials, or tax return records.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or broker financial data to lending aggregators, credit bureaus, or marketing networks.
  • We do NOT create shadow behavioral credit scores.

3. Minimal Technical & Operational Data

When you request a page from our website, standard web server logs may temporarily register:

  • Your internet protocol (IP) address (anonymized for geolocation country mapping).
  • Browser type, device architecture, operating system, and referral headers.
  • Timestamp of page requests to defend against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks.

4. Cookies & Google Consent Mode v2

We use minimal cookies strictly necessary to provide features you request:

  • Theme Cookie / LocalStorage: To remember whether you selected Night Mode, Day Mode, or Eye Protect Mode (xestus_theme_mode).
  • Consent State: To record your cookie preferences (xestus_consent_v2).
  • Analytics & Advertising: If enabled by you, non-intrusive aggregated analytical cookies help us understand page popularity to maintain free public access. We integrate Google Consent Mode v2 so tracking signals are never triggered without explicit user affirmative consent.

5. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act & GDPR

Under applicable data protection laws, you possess the right to:

  • Clear your local browser cache and localStorage at any time to erase local drafts.
  • Request clarification or lodge grievances regarding data privacy.
  • Refuse non-essential cookies via our Cookie Consent modal.
