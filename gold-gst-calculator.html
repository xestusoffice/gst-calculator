Gold Jewellery GST Calculation Formula

In India, gold jewellery billing has a specific two-part tax structure:

1. Raw Gold Tax (3% GST)

Gold Tax = (Gold Weight in Grams × Rate/g) × 0.03

Split: 1.5% CGST (Central Government) + 1.5% SGST (State Government).

2. Making Charges Tax (5% GST)

Making Charge Tax = Making Charges × 0.05

Total Bill: Raw Gold Price + Making Charges + 3% GST on Gold + 5% GST on Making Charges.
